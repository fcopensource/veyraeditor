**[Veyra PDF](/pdf) is now in the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/XP89HQ4DMX824K).** It's the same free PDF editor for Windows 10 and 11, now one click from the place Windows users already trust for apps.

## Why the Store

The direct download has always worked, but it wasn't signed with a Microsoft certificate, so Windows SmartScreen could show *"Windows protected your PC"* the first time you ran it. The Store version fixes that:

- **Signed and trusted by Windows.** It installs without warnings, like any other Store app.
- **Updates by itself.** New tools and fixes arrive through the Store, with nothing to download again.
- **Easy to find and remove.** It's in the Start menu and in *Settings → Apps*, like everything else from the Store.

The 2 MB installer is still on [the Veyra PDF page](/pdf) for anyone who prefers it.

## Every PDF tool, on your PC

Veyra PDF does what most people open an online PDF site for, without uploading anything:

- **Highlight and add text** in any size and colour.
- **Fill and sign:** draw your signature or type it in a handwriting style, then click to place it.
- **Combine** PDFs, JPGs and PNGs into one file, in the order you choose.
- **Organize pages:** drag them into order, rotate, delete, duplicate, insert blank pages, extract or split.
- **Watermarks and page numbers**, with a live preview.
- **Remove watermarks** added by a PDF editor, and **white-out** anything else.
- **Convert** photos and scans to PDF, PDF pages to JPG or PNG, and text to a `.txt` file.

## Your files stay on your computer

Online PDF tools ask you to upload bank statements, contracts and ID documents to someone else's server. Veyra PDF does all its work on your PC: no account, no upload, no tracking, and no watermark on your files. It works offline too.

## Get it

1. Open **[Veyra PDF in the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/XP89HQ4DMX824K)** and click **Get**.
2. Open it from the Start menu, or right-click any PDF and choose **Open with → Veyra PDF**.

Already using the installer version? You can keep it, or uninstall it from *Settings → Apps* and install the Store version to get automatic updates.

Veyra PDF is made by the team behind [Veyra Studio](/), the fast, free code editor. If you have an idea for a tool it should have, [tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/fcopensource/veyraeditor/issues).
