## Reddit: r/vscode

**Title:** I made a VS Code extension where your coding grows a garden (saving waters it, commits make flowers bloom)

**Text:**

> Pets in VS Code are great, but I wanted something that grows *with* my work instead of just keeping me company.
>
> Code Garden puts a little garden in the Explorer. Every minute you're actually typing helps the plants grow from
> seed to bloom, and a new seed sprouts every 20 minutes. Saving waters the soil (leave it for a day and the plants
> droop, but nothing ever dies). When you fix your last error, butterflies show up. Every commit makes your most grown
> plant burst into flower.
>
> There are nine species to collect: a tulip for 2 days in a row, a cactus for fixing 10 errors, a glowing mushroom
> for an hour of coding after 10 pm, a cherry tree for a 7-day streak, and a golden "Veyra Bloom" for 30 days. Day and
> night follow your clock, with fireflies at night.
>
> It's free, MIT licensed, about 60 KB, never reads your code and makes no network requests. You can save a captioned
> snapshot of your garden to share.
>
> Marketplace: https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=flightcoders.veyra-garden
> Source: https://github.com/fcopensource/veyraeditor/tree/main/extensions/garden
>
> What should the tenth plant be, and how should you unlock it?

## Hacker News (Show HN)

**Title:** Show HN: Code Garden – a VS Code garden that grows while you code

**URL:** https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=flightcoders.veyra-garden

**First comment:**

> A small, cozy thing I built for myself. It counts coding minutes (a minute where you typed in a file while VS Code was
> focused), saves, the moment your error count drops to zero, and commits from the built-in Git extension. Those drive a
> garden drawn on a canvas in a webview: plants grow through five stages, the soil dries over about ten hours and
> slows growth to half (it never kills anything), fixes bring butterflies, commits bloom the most grown plant.
>
> The rules live in one pure module with unit tests, and an integration test drives it inside real VS Code. Everything
> is drawn with canvas paths, no images. It never reads your code; the garden lives in globalState and syncs with
> Settings Sync.
>
> Source: https://github.com/fcopensource/veyraeditor/tree/main/extensions/garden
