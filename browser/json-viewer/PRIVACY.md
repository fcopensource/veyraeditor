# JSON Viewer Veyra: privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

JSON Viewer Veyra does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data or browsing data.

- **What it reads:** when a page you open is JSON, the extension reads that page's text in your browser to show it as a tree. It reads nothing on other pages.
- **What it stores:** your choice of view, indentation and theme, saved with Chrome's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which Chrome may sync to your Google account if you have sync turned on.
- **What it sends:** nothing. The extension makes no network requests and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- **JSON you paste or open** on the extension's own page stays in that tab and is gone when you close it.
