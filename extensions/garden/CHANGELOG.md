# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- A garden in the Explorer (and a bigger panel) that grows while you code: seed, sprout, leaves, bud and bloom.
- Saving waters the soil; dry gardens droop but never die. Fixing your last error brings butterflies; a commit makes your most grown plant bloom.
- Nine species to collect, unlocked by streaks, saves, fixes, commits and late-night coding, up to the golden Veyra Bloom.
- A full garden presses its oldest bloom into your collection to make room.
- Day and night follow your clock, with bees, fireflies, glowing mushrooms and falling cherry petals.
- Save a captioned snapshot of your garden to share.
- Your streak and blooms in the status bar.
- Private: never reads your code; the garden syncs with Settings Sync.
