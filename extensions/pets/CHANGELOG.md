# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Five pets drawn from scratch: cat, dog, fox, bunny and panda, in 14 colours.
- They walk, run, sit, nap, chase a ball you click or drag to throw, and show hearts when you pet them.
- Reactions to your coding: a hop on save, a worried look at new errors, a party when the last error is fixed, a nap while you're away.
- Meadow, night, beach and plain backgrounds; small, medium and large sizes.
- A Pets view in the Explorer, and a bigger panel where the pets grow to fit.
- Pets are saved and sync with Settings Sync.
