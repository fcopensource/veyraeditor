# Pets Veyra

**A little friend for your coding day.**

Pets Veyra puts cute pets in VS Code. A cat, a dog, a fox, a bunny or a panda walks around, naps, chases the ball you throw, and **reacts to your coding**: it cheers when you save, looks worried when errors appear, and throws a party when you fix the last one. **Free, and drawn from scratch.** By the makers of [Veyra Studio](https://veyraeditor.com).

- 🐾 **Five pets, many colours:** cats (orange, black, white, grey), dogs (brown, golden, black, white), foxes (red, arctic), bunnies (white, brown, grey) and a panda.
- 🎾 **Play with them:** click to pet them and see hearts; click the sky to drop a ball, or drag to throw it, and watch them race for it.
- 💻 **They follow your work:** a hop when you save, a worried "!" when errors appear, a party of stars when the last one is fixed, and a nap when you've been away for a while.
- 🌙 **Four places to live:** a sunny meadow, a starry night, the beach, or your theme's own background.

## Where to find them

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcopensource/veyraeditor/main/extensions/pets/media/readme/explorer.png" alt="The Pets view at the bottom of the Explorer, with buttons to throw a ball, add a pet and say goodbye" width="300">

- **In the Explorer:** the **Pets** view at the bottom of the Explorer. Drag it up for more room.
- **In a bigger panel:** **⋯ → Open in a Bigger Panel**, where the pets grow to fit.
- The buttons at the top of the view throw a ball, add a pet and say goodbye to one.

## Commands

| Command | |
|---|---|
| **Pets Veyra: Add a Pet…** | Choose a pet, its colour and its name |
| **Pets Veyra: Throw a Ball** | Fun for everyone |
| **Pets Veyra: Say Goodbye to a Pet…** | Remove one |
| **Pets Veyra: Rename a Pet…** | Give it a new name |
| **Pets Veyra: Change Background…** | Meadow, night, beach or plain |
| **Pets Veyra: Open in a Bigger Panel** | Bigger pets, beside your code |

Hover over a pet to see its name.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| `veyraPets.background` | `meadow` | `meadow`, `night`, `beach` or `plain` |
| `veyraPets.size` | `medium` | `small`, `medium` or `large` |
| `veyraPets.reactToCode` | `true` | Cheer on save, worry about new errors, party when they're fixed |
| `veyraPets.showNames` | `true` | Show a pet's name when you hover over it |

## Good to know

- Your pets are kept by VS Code and come with you to every window, and to your other computers with Settings Sync.
- They nap after 5 minutes without typing, and wake up when you're back.
- Nothing is sent anywhere, and the pets don't slow down your editor: they only move while you can see them.
- Pets Veyra is an independent extension. Every pet is drawn from scratch.
- Requires VS Code 1.85 or newer.
