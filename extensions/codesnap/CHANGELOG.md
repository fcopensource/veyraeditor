# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Snap the selection (Ctrl+Alt+S) into an image in your theme's colours.
- 10 gradients, solid or transparent backgrounds; macOS, Windows or no window; padding, shadow and real line numbers.
- Click lines to highlight them.
- Fit, square, 16:9 and LinkedIn sizes.
- Live updates as the selection changes.
- Copy image or save PNG at 1×, 2× or 3×, with your clipboard put back afterwards.
