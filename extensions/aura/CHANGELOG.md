# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Earn aura for clearing errors, clean saves, commits (and good commit messages), passing tests and builds, big deletes, locked-in sessions and late nights.
- Roast mode: lose a little for "wip" commit messages, failing tests and builds, force pushes and leftover debugger statements. Can be turned off.
- Eight ranks from NPC to Final Boss, a daily streak with a bonus, and daily limits so aura can't be farmed by spamming.
- Your aura and streak in the status bar, with each move flashing as it happens, and a notification when you rank up.
- The Aura panel: rank and progress, streak, the week, best moves, every move, the rules and the rank ladder.
- Share My Aura Card: a 1200 × 630 image and a caption copied to the clipboard.
- Commits from VS Code's Git, tests and builds from tasks and terminal commands (with shell integration). 100% local.
