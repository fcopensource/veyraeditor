## 1.0.0

First release of Neon Getaway, an open-world driving game in VS Code.

- Solara Bay: four districts, traffic in lanes, people on the sidewalks, the sea, a pier and 20 hidden mixtapes.
- Take any car with E. Arcade handling with handbrake drifts, and four cars to buy at Rico's Garage.
- Wanted stars, police that chase you through the streets, losing them, the spray shop, and getting busted.
- Six story jobs, a street race, taxi fares, a minimap and a city map.
- A synthwave radio and engine sounds made live with Web Audio.
- Your code pays: $25 of in-game cash for each saved file, up to $1,000 a day.
