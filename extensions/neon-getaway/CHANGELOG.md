## 1.0.0

First release of Neon Getaway, a retro neon driving game in VS Code.

- Solara Bay: four districts, traffic in lanes, people on the sidewalks, the sea, a pier and 20 hidden mixtapes.
- Arcade handling with handbrake drifts, and four cars to buy at Rico's Garage.
- Six story missions, a street race, taxi fares, a minimap and a city map.
- A synthwave radio and engine sounds made live with Web Audio.
- A small in-game bonus for each saved file, with a daily limit.
