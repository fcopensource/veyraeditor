# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Word documents (.docx, .docm, .dotx) shown as pages, with a heading outline and page and word counts.
- PowerPoint decks (.pptx, .pptm, .ppsx) with theme colours, layouts and masters, pictures, tables, speaker notes and a full-screen Present mode.
- Excel workbooks (.xlsx, .xlsm) with number, currency, percent and date formats, merged cells, fills, sheet tabs and a formula bar.
- CSV and TSV files as a sortable, filterable table with Open as Table.
- Find, zoom, Copy text and Open in app for every file.
- Older .doc, .ppt and .xls files open with an explanation and an Open in default app button.
