# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Your Git history as an animated story: commits, lines, coding hours, favourite day, streaks, languages, files, favourite words and your biggest commit
- Eight coding personalities, from the Night Owl to the Steady Shipper
- A share card to save as PNG or copy, at 2400 × 1260
- This year, the last 12 months or all time; just you or the whole team
- Lockfiles, builds and minified files are left out of your line counts
- Runs your own Git on your computer: nothing is uploaded
